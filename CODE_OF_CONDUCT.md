# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, useful place for people who are in the middle of a stressful search.

## Our standard

Be kind and specific. Assume good intent, give feedback on the work and not the person, and respect privacy: never post
someone else's records, messages or personal details, and keep your own redacted. Harassment, discrimination, personal
attacks and spam are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn or ban people who break this standard. To report a problem, use
GitHub's private reporting on the repository or contact a maintainer through the profile linked in the README. Reports are
handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
