# Contributing

This repo is the system only. Anything that describes a person (a name, an email, a company you targeted, a posting,
a message, a score, a result, your writing voice) belongs in your own data folder, not here.

## Before you commit
1. `python3 scripts/kernel/sync.py` if you changed a card, skill, lane or hook; commit the regenerated files.
2. `python3 scripts/doctor.py` is green (warnings are fine, failures are not).
3. `python3 -m pytest scripts -m "not real_records and not workspace" -q` passes on the demo data.
4. `python3 tools/leak_check.py --staged` prints no findings. The hook installed by `./install.sh` runs it for you.

## Rules
- Personal values go through `scripts/agency_config.py` with neutral defaults in `config/defaults.d/`, a synthetic
  value in `scripts/tests/persona.d/`, and your real value in your data folder's `config/`.
- Tests and fixtures use synthetic people and companies (`example.test` addresses, "Acme Corp"). Public ATS and platform
  names are fine; real employers, candidates and postings are not.
- Prose in cards, skills and playbooks describes method. No incident stories, no dated rulings, no quotes of a person,
  no voice rules of your own: those are config.
- Generated files carry a `GENERATED by scripts/kernel/sync.py` header; edit the source and re-run sync.
- One writer per artifact; an agent that finds a problem outside its files reports it.
- Commits: plain messages, one author.
