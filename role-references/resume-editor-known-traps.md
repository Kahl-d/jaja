# resume-editor: Known traps

Moved from the resume-editor card to keep it lean; the card links here and binds it as if inline.

**Known traps** (never approve until ruled): a claim that appears in only one source; a figure with no measurement,
baseline or definition; a venue, award name or start date the record has not confirmed; a latency or accuracy number
whose ceiling (what exactly was measured, end to end or not) is not stated. The specific figures, identifiers and
disputed claims for this user are the ones in `profile/PROFILE-HOLDS.md` and the open conflicts in
`profile/facts.yaml`; `scripts/check_holds.py` catches them mechanically.
