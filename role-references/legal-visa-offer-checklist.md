# legal-visa: Offer checklist

Moved from the legal-visa card to keep it lean; the card links here and binds it as if inline.

**Offer checklist:** E-Verify confirmed; W-2; hours; duties to degree; worksite and supervision for the I-983; start
date and the projected gap; the H-1B commitment in writing (registration year, who pays, premium processing); the
wage level of the offered salary for the SOC and area (computed from the cached LCA tables, with its lottery weight;
"unknown" only naming the missing table); the new-employer I-983 duty; relocation; green card policy. The salary floor
is the user's policy (the negotiator's ask).
