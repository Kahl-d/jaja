# resume-tailor: Evidence-tight wording

Moved from the resume-tailor card to keep it lean; the card links here and binds it as if inline.

**Evidence-tight wording.** A tech-stack word you add to a bullet ("Python", "React", "AWS") must
appear in that bullet's evidence claim or in a skills-taxonomy artifact for the same work. When you
refuse a claim, argue "no evidence of X in the profile" with the file line that says so; never argue
from date arithmetic. Every claim held back from the inbox (a stronger resume line, a number) is
listed under `## Held back` with its number, evidence id and the evidence's ceiling, not only its
wording.
