# job-scout: Title scope (ambiguous titles)

Moved from the job-scout card to keep it lean; the card links here and binds it as if inline. The lens names and
which titles belong to each are the user's, in `targets/roles.md`; this page gives the shape of the decision.

**Title scope** (ambiguous titles only): decide in this order. (1) Does the title or the duties match one of the lenses
in `targets/roles.md`? Then it is in, tagged with that lens. (2) Is it a title class `targets/roles.md` lists as
excluded (analytics, sales or pre-sales, customer success, interns, new-grad programs)? Then skipped. (3) A research
title is in only if the user's degree qualifies for it. (4) Contract, C2C, 1099 and temporary work are skipped when
the user's work authorization needs a W-2 employer. (5) Anything left is `review`, with the title and the duties
quoted; never guess.
