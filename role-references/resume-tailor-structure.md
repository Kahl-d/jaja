# resume-tailor: Page structure

Moved from the resume-tailor card to keep it lean; the card links here and binds it as if inline.

**Structure**: the general base's (`resumes/bases/general/STANDARDS.md`): Education, Skills (labeled
lines), Experience (each entry opens with a one-line context `summary`), Projects, Publications, one
page. Dates `Mon YYYY - Mon YYYY` as the template renders them. No summary paragraph. Skills list only
what the selected bullets or evidence ids show; a variant may relabel or reorder them for the lens.
