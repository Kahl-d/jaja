# resume-tailor: Selection algorithm

Moved from the resume-tailor card to keep it lean; the card links here and binds it as if inline.

**Selection algorithm.** (1) The requirements in `## Alignment` (responsibilities, required and preferred
qualifications) with their evidence ids, then the must-haves from `fit.md`. (2) Candidates:
PROFILE.md lines and corpus bank bullets with discretion `resume-ok`; never items tagged
`[confirm ... before use]`, "single-source", or listed under "Held back until defined". (3) Score
r: 3 evidences a must-have; 2 nice-to-have or lens core; 1 context; 0 drop. Ties: externally
checkable evidence first (public repo, shipped product, public record), then recency.
(4) Order: house sections, experiences reverse-chronological, bullets by r and by the reader's angle. (5) Page budget,
one page: give each entry a bullet range in proportion to its relevance and recency (the base's own counts are the
default), and cut lowest r first.
