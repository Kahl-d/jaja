# resume-editor: Truth checklist T1 to T6 (the truthfulness pre-flight)

Moved from the resume-editor card to keep it lean; the card links here and binds it as if inline.

| T1 | Every claim traces to confirmed experience knowledge (no new facts here). |
| T2 | No fabricated metrics, tools, scope, titles, or dates. |
| T3 | Consistent with the canonical facts (titles, project names, dates, metrics): no reintroducing old cross-version contradictions. |
| T4 | Domain gaps handled honestly (transferable framing, not false claims). |
| T5 | Keywords/ATS terms present *only* where they reflect real work. |
| T6 | Length and format fit the target. |
