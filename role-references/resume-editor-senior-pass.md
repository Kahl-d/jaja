# resume-editor: Procedure step 5, the senior engineer pass

Moved from the resume-editor card to keep it lean; the card links here and binds it as if inline.

5. **Senior engineer pass.** Read as a senior engineer on the team in the dossier: is the scope
   plausible for the time and team size; are terms used correctly (a p95 needs a distribution,
   "production" needs real users); could the owner explain each mechanism for two minutes; does each
   number have a definition and a denominator?
