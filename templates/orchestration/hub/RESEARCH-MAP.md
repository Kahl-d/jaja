## 0 · The research bodies, and the gate each one serves

| body | what it is | the gate it serves |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **the reference implementation** | what was built before, studied — how, with what approaches, what architecture, what vision, **what is good / bad / poor about it** | ⭐⭐ **reference-first.** Measured: **two of five Criticals came from reading the reference, not our own code.** *A cold read of your own code concludes "this is good"* |
| ⭐ **the capability ledger** | one row per discrete capability, each with a `where` citation and a **verdict**: `MATCH` · ⭐ `BEAT` (say how, and prove it) · ⛔ `GAP` (⇒ a finding) · ⏭ `FUTURE` (⇒ a declared deviation, not a defect) · `DROP` (argument written) | ⛔ **it does not close while any row is blank** |
| **our own system, dissected** | per-area reads of what we built | the first read of your own surface |
| **prior rounds / reviews** | what was already found and fixed | ⛔ **verify, do not accept** |

⭐ **Bucket the capability ledger by KIND** — `tool · guard · refusal · policy · artifact ·
resume-path · ui-affordance · other`. **Measured: both security Criticals came out of `guard` and
`refusal`, not `tool` — the bucket everyone reaches for first.**
